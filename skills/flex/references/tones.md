# Tone reference

Seven tones. Each changes scripting energy, pacing, typography personality, and transition style.

## `default`
Energy: Playful, clean, postable. The product gets to be funny on its own terms.
Voice: First person plural. Warm. Direct. No corporate language.

## `polished`
Energy: Serious, elegant. Uses restraint as the creative choice.
Voice: Third person or no voice. The product speaks for itself.

## `yc-parody`
Energy: Deadpan startup launch energy. The joke is how seriously it's delivered.
Voice: Serious. Matter-of-fact. No winking.

## `chaotic`
Energy: Fast, loud, aggressive. Over-the-top.
Voice: ALL CAPS energy when it helps. Rapid cuts.

## `deadpan`
Energy: Calm, dry, understated. The joke is that nothing is a joke.
Voice: Minimal. Long holds. Quiet confidence.

## `cinematic`
Energy: Dramatic, trailer-scale. Big motion, bigger claims.
Voice: Epic but still specific to the product.

## `app-store`
Energy: Smooth, feature-card clean. Corporate but not boring.
Voice: Clear benefit statements. Clean slides.

Presets are defaults, not limits. Freeform direction always wins when the user gives it.
