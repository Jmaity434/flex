AI SaaS launch video generator — product demo videos from your website or code

flex is an open-source AI agent skill that turns any SaaS product, startup landing page, or app into a short, polished product launch video — music, motion, and social share copy included. One command. No video editor.

Install the launch video skill

Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Google Antigravity, opencode, and other AI coding agents.

npx skills add https://github.com/Jmaity434/flex --skill flex

Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add Jmaity434/flex
/plugin install flex@flex

Generate a SaaS product launch video

let's /flex
/flex --tone polished --format vertical
/flex --voice
/flex https://your-saas-site.com
/flex doctor

Output: a ready-to-share SaaS marketing video (flex.mp4), poster frame, and captions for X, LinkedIn, and Instagram.

FAQ — AI SaaS launch videos

What is an AI SaaS launch video generator?

flex is an open-source AI agent skill that reads your SaaS product or website and generates a short, polished product launch video with music, motion, and social share copy in one command.

Can flex create a product demo video from a website URL?

Yes. Point /flex at a live URL. It deeply scans the page (including JavaScript-rendered sites), extracts brand, UI, and copy, then builds a launch video.

Does flex work with Claude Code and Cursor?

Yes. flex works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Google Antigravity, opencode, and other agents that support the Agent Skills standard.

Is flex free for commercial SaaS marketing videos?

Yes. MIT licensed — free for commercial SaaS launches and client work.
