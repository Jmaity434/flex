How to create a SaaS launch video with AI (practical guide)

Updated for 2026 · Open-source tool: flex

A strong SaaS launch video shows what the product does in under 30 seconds, uses real UI, and is ready to post on Product Hunt, LinkedIn, X, and Reels. Hiring an editor is slow. With an AI agent skill like flex, you can generate a product launch video from your website or codebase in one command.

1. What makes a good SaaS product launch video

  • Length: 15–25 seconds (attention-friendly)
  • Hook in the first 2 seconds
  • Real product UI — not only abstract animations
  • Specific claims from your landing page (no generic “streamline your workflow”)
  • Formats: landscape for LinkedIn/X, vertical for Reels/Shorts

2. Generate a launch video from your SaaS site

Install flex, then from your project folder or with a URL:

npx skills add https://github.com/Jmaity434/flex --skill flex

# from your SaaS repo
let's /flex

# or from a live landing page
/flex https://your-saas.com --format vertical --tone polished

flex deep-scans the site (including JavaScript-heavy apps), extracts brand colors, fonts, and copy, and produces flex.mp4 plus share captions for social platforms.

3. Website-to-video vs code-based product demos

Website to video: best when you only have a marketing URL. Code-based: best when the agent can read real components and flows for a more accurate product demo video. flex supports both and stays stack-agnostic (React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte, plain HTML, and more).

4. Vertical reels for SaaS marketing

/flex --vertical-first
/flex --formats landscape,vertical

Export both a LinkedIn-friendly landscape clip and a vertical SaaS marketing video for Shorts and Reels in one run.
